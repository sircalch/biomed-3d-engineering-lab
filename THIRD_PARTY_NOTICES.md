- Three.js: WebGL rendering library used for the 3D viewer. License: MIT. Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/LICENSE
- Health Icons: selected SVG icons included in `public/assets/health-icons`. License: CC0 for icons; website MIT. Source: https://healthicons.org/
